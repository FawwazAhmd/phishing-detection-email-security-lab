# Phishing Email 01 — Indicators of Compromise

## Sender
- Email address: security@micr0soft-security.example
- Suspicious element: Lookalike spelling of "Microsoft" using "0" instead of "o"

## Subject
- "Urgent: Your Microsoft 365 account will be suspended"
- Indicator: Urgency and account-suspension threat

## URL
- https://login-microsoft-security.example/verify
- Suspicious element: Non-Microsoft domain impersonating a Microsoft security service

## Phishing Indicators
- Brand impersonation
- Lookalike domain
- Urgency
- Threat of account suspension
- Request for credential/account verification

## Initial Assessment
The email exhibits multiple characteristics commonly associated with
credential-phishing attempts, including brand impersonation, a
lookalike sender domain, urgency and a suspicious verification URL.
